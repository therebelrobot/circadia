# Soil moisture

Measured as volumetric water content (VWC). Fruit trees here want roughly 20–30% VWC in
summer; below ~15% the drip lines should open. Readings drift with soil temperature, so
calibrate per probe (see [[calibrate-moisture-sensor]]).
